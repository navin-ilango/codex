# Technical leadership and behavioral questions

Tell me about a technical decision you made that significantly improved reliability, scalability, or cost.



Describe a time you disagreed with another senior engineer about an architectural decision. How did you resolve it?



How do you lead an initiative when the engineers involved do not report to you?



Tell me about a time a project was at risk of missing its deadline. What did you do?



How do you balance technical debt, platform reliability, feature delivery, and cost optimization?



How do you decide which technical problems deserve immediate attention and which can wait?



Tell me about a time you mentored an engineer who was struggling with a complex technical problem.



How do you evaluate whether an engineer is ready for more technical ownership or a promotion?



How do you handle a team that repeatedly ignores platform standards or operational best practices?



Describe a technical initiative that failed or did not produce the expected outcome. What did you learn?



How do you communicate technical risk to leadership without overwhelming them with implementation details?



How would you handle a disagreement with your engineering manager about reliability versus delivery deadlines?



How do you maintain a high technical bar while making hiring decisions under time pressure?



What would you do if a highly productive engineer repeatedly bypassed security or deployment controls?



What would your first 90 days look like as a Lead Engineer joining a new organization?
