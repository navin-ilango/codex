# Cloud Computing

1. `On Demand Service` - Can provision capabilities as needed without requiring human integration.
2. `Broad Network Acces` - Capabilities are available over the network and access through the standard mechanisms.
3. `Resource Poolomg` - There is sense of location independence and no control or knowledge over the exact location of the resources. Resources are Pooled to serve multiple consumers using a multi-tenant model. 
4. `Rapid Elacticity` - Capabilities can be elastically provisioned and releases to scale rapidle outward and inward with demand. To the consumers, the capabilities available for provisioning often appear to be unlimited.
5. `Measured Service` - Resource usage can be monitored, controlled, reported and billed.

`Cloud Types`
1. PUBLIC - Available for general public and meets the 5 cloud required characteristics.
2. PRIVATE - Dedicated to your business and runs from your premises.
3. HYBRID - Public and Private cloud used in conjunction.
4. MULTI - Multiple piblic clouds.

`Cloud Service Models`
1. IAAS - Ex: EC2
2. PAAS - Ex: RDS, Heruku, Lambda.
3. SAAS - Ex: Gmail, Netflixm, Dropbox
